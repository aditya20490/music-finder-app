How to see 3D

  1. Rotate your phone to landscape.
  2. Look far away, like at a mountain peak. Don't focus on the screen.
  3. Hold the phone in front of you and slowly shift your focus closer to the screen.
  4. At one point it clicks and the picture pops into 3D.
  5. Guide dot: each picture has a white dot at the top. When the two dots merge into one, the pictures are locked together.

Camera 3D tips

  1. Pick a point and keep it in the green circle.
  2. Take shot 1, then slide the phone 5-8 cm sideways.
  3. Match the faded ghost image and don't tilt or move up/down.

Video 3D tips

  1. Film in landscape and slide the phone slowly and steadily sideways. No sideways movement means no 3D.
  2. Tell the app which way you moved (right or left). If it looks inside out, switch it.
  3. Use the 3D strength slider. Faster movement needs a smaller gap.
  4. Tap Save video to render it. This takes as long as the video itself.

Stereogram tips

  1. Type a message or pick a photo. The hidden shape is invisible in the normal view.
  2. Two white dots are at the top. Look far away and let them merge into one dot, then keep your eyes relaxed. The hidden shape slowly appears.
  3. If it looks like a hole instead of raised, switch Parallel / Cross-eye.
  4. If it's hard to lock on, try the Pattern size slider.
  5. For Hidden photo, use the Subject cutoff slider to grow or shrink the hidden subject.

Library

  1. Every picture you make is kept in the Library tab automatically. Videos are kept once you save or share them.
  2. Open one and tap Delete to remove it from the app and from your gallery.
